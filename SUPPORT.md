For changes to these shared guidelines or the organization profile, open a [pull request in the `.github` repository](https://github.com/regen-coordination/.github/pulls).

## Ask a useful question

Describe what you are trying to do, the relevant project or resource, what you expected, and what happened. Include links or a small example when useful. Remove personal information and secrets from screenshots, logs, and other public attachments.

For substantial new work, describe the intended outcome and ask maintainers about scope before starting. See [Contributing](https://github.com/regen-coordination/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for the submission process and [Governance](https://github.com/regen-coordination/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) for decision routes.

## Private concerns

- **Security vulnerabilities:** Email [regencoordination@proton.me](mailto:regencoordination@proton.me) and follow the [Security policy](https://github.com/regen-coordination/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- **Conduct incidents:** Email [regencoordination@proton.me](mailto:regencoordination@proton.me) and follow the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/regen-coordination/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Keep sensitive details out of public issues and pull requests.
